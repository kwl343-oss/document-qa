✨ 9 NEW INVESTOR-GRADE FEATURES

1. 📋 DEAL SNAPSHOT
   └─ Executive summary card with all key metrics at a glance
   └─ Company, stage, sector, raise, ARR, growth, runway, materials

2. 🎯 RECOMMENDATION (VC Decision)
   └─ Clear INVEST / WATCHLIST / PASS badge
   └─ Confidence level (High/Med/Low)
   └─ Combines model probability + investor profile fit

3. 📊 THESIS FIT SCORE
   └─ 0–100 personalized score matching YOUR preferences
   └─ Stage/sector/revenue matching indicators
   └─ Red flag detection (e.g., low runway)

4. ✨ KEY EXTRACTED FACTS
   └─ Auto-extracts: business model, ICP, pricing, GTM, traction, moat
   └─ Saves 30 minutes of manual deck reading
   └─ Uses GPT-4o-mini with constrained JSON

5. 🧠 MODEL REASONING (Explainability)
   └─ Step-by-step breakdown of scoring
   └─ "Boost: Strong growth rate (18% is solid)"
   └─ "Match: This aligns with your preferred stage"

6. ❓ FOUNDER DILIGENCE QUESTIONS
   └─ Auto-generates 8–12 specific questions
   └─ Tailored to stage, sector, missing metrics, detected risks
   └─ Ready to send or print for founder calls

7. 📋 IC MEMO GENERATOR
   └─ Auto-drafts full investment committee memo
   └─ Sections: Overview, Market, Traction, Team, Risks, Thesis, Recommendation
   └─ 80% done; just customize and send

8. 💬 COPILOT QUICK ACTIONS
   └─ 6 buttons for instant prompts:
      • Summarize deck (8 bullets)
      • List risks (top 5-8)
      • Extract traction metrics
      • Draft founder follow-up
      • Write IC summary
      • Suggest next steps

9. 🎨 PREMIUM VISUAL DESIGN

🎬 QUICK WORKFLOW (5 minutes per deal)

  1. Upload deck (PDF/PPTX)
  2. Click "🤖 Extract fields" (auto-populates)
  3. Click "▶ Run VCaaS analysis"
  4. Review scorecard:
     ├─ Deal Snapshot (metrics)
     ├─ Recommendation (INVEST/WATCHLIST/PASS)
     ├─ Thesis Fit Score (if profile set)
     └─ Model Reasoning (why this score)
  5. Generate artifacts:
     ├─ "✨ Extract facts" → business highlights
     ├─ "❓ Founder questions" → 12 diligence questions
     └─ "📋 IC memo" → full memo
  6. Chat with copilot or use quick actions
